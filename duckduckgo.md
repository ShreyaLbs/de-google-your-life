# DuckDuckGo (alternative to Google Search)

**What it is:** A search engine that does not track your searches or build a profile of you.

**Why switch:**
- No search history tied to you
- Less personalised ad targeting
- Familiar, fast results

**Install / set up:** https://duckduckgo.com (set as default search engine in your browser; the DuckDuckGo browser app is also available at https://duckduckgo.com/app)

**Quick start:**
1. In Firefox: Settings > Search > Default Search Engine > DuckDuckGo
2. Search the same query on Google and DuckDuckGo and compare the results
3. Try the `!g` bang to fall back to Google when you need it

**Things to know:** Results can differ from Google for very local or very niche queries.
