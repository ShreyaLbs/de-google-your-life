# De-Google Your Life 🔓

> 5 apps you already know, reinvented — free, open, yours.

A hands-on workshop by the **FOSS Club, LBS Institute of Technology for Women** exploring free and open-source alternatives to everyday big-tech tools.

## The 5 Swaps

| Instead of | Use | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp | Signal | [tools/signal.md](tools/signal.md) |
| MS Office | LibreOffice | [tools/libreoffice.md](tools/libreoffice.md) |
| Chrome | Firefox | [tools/firefox.md](tools/firefox.md) |
| Google Drive | Nextcloud / Syncthing | [tools/nextcloud-syncthing.md](tools/nextcloud-syncthing.md) |
| Google Search | DuckDuckGo | [tools/duckduckgo.md](tools/duckduckgo.md) |

## Try It Yourself
Follow the [checklist](checklist.md) and swap at least 3 tools.
